DAIMA Health Managing System

One patient record from reception to pharmacy, billing and reporting. Use it in your browser, or install it on your phone.

Recommended for your computer

Use it on the web

Works in any modern browser on a computer, tablet or phone. Nothing to install.

  1. Open the web app.
  2. Choose your department portal and sign in.
Open the web app
Recommended for your phone

Android app

Download the APK and install it directly. It opens full screen with its own icon.

  1. Tap Download APK and wait for it to finish.
  2. Open daima-health.apk from your notifications or Downloads.
  3. If Android asks, tap Settings and allow your browser to install unknown apps, then go back.
  4. Tap Install, then Open.
Download APK

Needs Android 5.0 or newer, and Chrome for the best experience.

Recommended for your iPhone

iPhone & iPad

Add DAIMA Health to your Home Screen. It opens full screen, just like an app.

  1. Tap Open in Safari below.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen (top right on iPad).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add.
Open in Safari

The app also shows these steps when you open it on an iPhone.

Having trouble? Use the web version. It has every feature the apps do.
